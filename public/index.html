Rights-first defaults

Every publish step is designed to protect the creator.

  • No watermark removal or identity impersonation.
  • Only owned, licensed, or explicitly authorized footage can be rendered.
  • Music requires title, license, and permission confirmation.
  • Auto-publish stays disabled unless explicitly approved.
  • Public posting requires a review state and a live account connection.
