Rights-first defaults

Built to reduce copyright risk and over-publishing.

  • Ownership confirmation is required before a project may render.
  • Music requires a title, a license and explicit permission.
  • Auto-publish stays disabled unless the creator explicitly enables it.
  • No watermark removal, identity impersonation, or third-party likeness reuse.
  • Platform publishing only occurs after a verified account connection and approval state.
